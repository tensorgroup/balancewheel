Moderated multi-model review

Other vendors' agents review your work. The results choose your reviewers.

You work in one agent, the lead. At a checkpoint it asks other vendors' agents, the seats, to review. The seats can read your code. They cannot change it. The lead checks every finding in the code and logs the result. The log becomes a scoreboard.

    Leads and rotation

    When a plan runs out, work goes on

    A profile lists your leads in order. When the current lead's plan is capped, bw stops it at its next check, writes a handoff, and starts the next lead. A plan that is only low never moves a running lead. After the preferred plan resets, your next session goes back to it. The plans and times here are an illustration.
